We are going to build a small knowledge base in the folder hypoxia_kb/. Read
hypoxia_kb/README.md first: it describes the folder, the card format and the rules. Follow
it exactly.

If you need Python packages, create a virtual environment in this folder first
(python3 -m venv .venv) and install them there. Do not install anything system-wide.

The papers are in hypoxia_kb/sources/:
  Hofner2025_KGN_hypoxia.md   - a proteomics study of human granulosa cells (KGN) in low oxygen
  Luo2022_hypoxia_review.md   - a review of hypoxia signalling
Read them with tools. Do not print them in full.

My differential proteomics results are in ../1_proteomics/results.csv. If that file does not
exist, use results_reference.csv in this folder instead (an analysis of the same experiment).
Copy the file you use to hypoxia_kb/my_results.csv, and tell me which one you used.

Keep a file called NOTES.md in this folder (not inside hypoxia_kb/) and update it after
every step with the current state and the next step.


1. PROPOSE - then stop
   Propose 5 concept cards: processes, mechanisms or methods that both papers help to
   explain (for example, how cells sense oxygen).
   Propose 6 gene cards, chosen from MY significant proteins in my_results.csv
   (q < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 1):
     - 4 that the papers discuss: the two most up and the two most down among those the
       papers mention. If fewer than two went down, say so and take more from the up side.
     - 2 that neither paper mentions.
   To decide whether a paper mentions a gene, search the papers for its symbol AND for the
   protein's other common names. Show me what you searched for and how many hits you got.
   Show me both lists, and wait until I type "continue".

2. WRITE
   One file per card: concepts in hypoxia_kb/concepts/, genes in hypoxia_kb/genes/. The
   file name is the card name. Gene cards are named by the gene symbol exactly as it
   appears in my_results.csv.

   Use exactly the format in hypoxia_kb/README.md. In particular:
   - Under "What the papers say", every claim is one bullet ending in its source and
     section, for example (Hofner2025, Results). On the next line, indented, quote the
     exact sentence from the paper that supports it:
         - NDRG1 is more abundant in KGN cells in hypoxia. (Hofner2025, Results)
           > "The more abundantly expressed proteins included AK4 ..., and NDRG1 ..."
     Copy the sentence from the paper - do not retype or paraphrase it. You may leave words
     out with "...". Quotes must be at least 6 words long.
   - Anything from your general knowledge, not from these papers, ends in
     (not from these sources) and has no quote.
   - Gene cards: put my log2FC and q in the front matter as my_log2FC and my_q, copied from
     my_results.csv. Under "My result", say in words what I found. Under "Do my result and
     the papers agree?", say agree, disagree or not reported, and why the results could
     differ.
   - For the 2 genes the papers do not mention: do not make up claims to fill the card.
     Under "What the papers say", write one bullet saying neither paper mentions it, marked
     (not from these sources). The definition can come from general knowledge, marked the
     same way.
   - Write about the concept or gene, not about the paper.
   - Link each card to at least two others with [[exact card name]], each with a few words
     saying why.

3. INDEX
   Write hypoxia_kb/INDEX.md: every card as a [[link]], grouped by type, one line each.

4. CHECK - run the script, fix, run again
   Run:   python hypoxia_kb/tools/check_kb.py
   It writes hypoxia_kb/CHECK_REPORT.md. Fix every ERROR:
   - a quote that is not found: find the real sentence in the paper and use it. If no
     sentence supports the claim, delete the claim.
   - record every claim you deleted or changed in hypoxia_kb/CHANGELOG.md, with the reason.
   Do not change the script, and do not remove a claim just to make a warning go away.
   Run the check again. Stop after three rounds even if errors remain.

5. REPORT
   Tell me: how many quotes the script found word for word in the first round and in the
   last round, what you had to change or delete, and any warnings that remain. Then run
       python make_zip.py
   and tell me the file it made.
