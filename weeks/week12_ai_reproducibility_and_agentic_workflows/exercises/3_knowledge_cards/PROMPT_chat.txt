Rules:
- Under "What the papers say", every claim is one bullet ending in its source and section,
  for example (Hofner2025, Results). On the next line, indented, the exact sentence from
  the paper, starting with > and in quotes. Copy it, do not paraphrase it. You may leave
  words out with "...". At least 6 words.
- Anything from general knowledge ends in (not from these sources) and has no quote.
- Gene cards: my_log2FC and my_q in the front matter, copied from results.csv. For the gene
  the papers do not mention, do not invent claims.

- Never invent a source.

Output format - this matters, because a script unpacks it. Put every file in ONE code
block per answer, and start each file with a line like this:
